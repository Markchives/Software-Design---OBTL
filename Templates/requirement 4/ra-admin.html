Access Policy

Raw assessment scores are owned by the academic workflow. Faculty enter and edit scores for their assigned courses and sections; Department Heads review scores without editing. Admin does not have score-entry or score-editing privileges.

System-level visibility: use audit logs and error records in Administration to investigate score-module issues. Do not modify academic score records from this account. This prototype does not expose actual score values on this page.
