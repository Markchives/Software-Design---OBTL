HELP & SUPPORT

Frequently asked questions

What is OBTL?

Outcome-Based Teaching and Learning aligns teaching and assessment with clearly defined learning outcomes.

What is the difference between CO and SO?

Course Outcomes are course-specific expectations. Student Outcomes represent broader competencies developed across courses.

What does CQI mean?

Continuous Quality Improvement uses learning evidence to identify gaps, plan interventions, and evaluate their impact.

Can I submit actual grades here?

EduMetrics is a student-project prototype. Use demonstration data unless your institution has explicitly approved a secure production deployment.
