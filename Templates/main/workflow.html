ILLUSTRATIVE CLASSROOM SCENARIO

What if an outcome misses its target?

Imagine a course where SO3 reaches only 58% attainment. The workflow connects that result to a concrete teaching response.

  1. Review mapped assessment evidence
  2. Identify the learning gap
  3. Plan targeted practice and rubric changes
  4. Collect new evidence next term

CQI STORY / ILLUSTRATIVE EXAMPLE

From a missed target to a better learning plan

Suppose SO3 reaches 58% attainment, below the course's configured target. This is a fictional classroom example, not a reported EduMetrics result.

  1. Review evidence. Faculty examine which mapped assessments contributed to SO3 and verify the scoring records.
  2. Find the gap. Review item-level patterns, rubric criteria, and activities to understand where learners struggled.
  3. Plan action. Document targeted practice, clearer rubrics, or revised instructional activities in the CQI plan.
  4. Measure again. Collect comparable evidence next term and evaluate whether the intervention improved attainment.
