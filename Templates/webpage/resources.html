Frequently asked questions

Key concepts users should know before working with assessment data.

What is a Course Outcome?

A measurable statement describing what students should be able to demonstrate after completing a course.

Why map assessments to COs?

Mapping connects assessment evidence to intended outcomes, making attainment analysis more traceable.

What is a flagged CO?

A Course Outcome whose measured attainment falls below the configured target and needs review.

What is CQI?

Continuous Quality Improvement uses evidence and analysis to identify and document actions for improvement.
