Signing In

Use the login page for your assigned EduMetrics role and enter the account credentials connected to your profile.

✉

Email Verification

Enter the verification code sent to your email address. You can request a new code after the resend timer ends.

Password Help

Check that your password meets the required format. If you cannot remember it, use the available password recovery option.

✓

Account Access

If your account is locked, inactive, or still cannot be accessed, contact your authorized EduMetrics administrator for assistance.
