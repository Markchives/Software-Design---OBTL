QUICK ANSWERS

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I verify my EduMetrics account?

Enter the verification code sent during the account verification process, then select Verify.

Where can I find my syllabus and assessments?

After logging in, open Workflow and select Syllabus & Outcomes to view available course materials and assigned assessments.

Why is an assessment not showing on my account?

Assessments appear when they are assigned and made available for your account. Check the relevant course and assessment status.

What should I do if I cannot log in?

Check your login information and verification status. If access problems continue, use the report form beside this section.

Who should I contact if my course information is incorrect?

Report the issue through the support form and include the affected course, section, or account details.
