Using EduMetrics

Find guidance for syllabus, CO/SO assessments, grading, workflow tools, and course features.

01

Syllabus & Outcomes

View course syllabus information and access Course Outcome and Student Outcome activities available for your role.

02

CO/SO Assessments

Open assigned CO/SO assessments, complete the required responses, and review available assessment information.

03

Grading & Attainment

Use the grading and attainment areas available to your account to review course assessment and outcome information.

04

Workflow Tools

Access EduMetrics workflow tools from the sidebar, including curriculum mapping, class roster, assessment entry, CQI, and reports.

05

My Courses

Open your assigned courses to view the course information and features connected to your EduMetrics account.

06

Navigation

Use the sidebar to move between Home, My Courses, Platform, Workflow, Resources, and other available pages.
